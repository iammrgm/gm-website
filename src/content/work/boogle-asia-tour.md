## What changed

The tour was possible because of a different tour.
I ran Asia in 2017 as business manager for a UK artist — Seoul, Tokyo, Shanghai, Hong Kong, nine days across five territories. Every promoter relationship, every venue contact and the Yeti Out partnership all date from then. Seven years is a long payback period on a network. It's also the only reason a London members' club could get into the right rooms in Seoul and Tokyo at all, rather than the rooms that take bookings.
The second thing: I'd have weighted the whole thing differently around the anchor events. Seoul during Blockchain Week and Singapore against Breakpoint did the heavy lifting because the audience was already in the city. Tokyo was the best night of the four and the hardest to fill, because nothing else was drawing people there that week. Cultural credibility is built in the small room. Numbers come from the week the city is already busy.

## Outcome

205% increase in event registrations against previous BOOGLE events.
